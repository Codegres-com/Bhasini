Summary

  • Bhasini has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and needs no account. The only accounts are for the paid Translation API, which keeps your email address, credit balance, usage counts and payment records.
  • Text sent to Bhasini's tools is converted in memory and discarded when the request ends. We do not store it, log it, read it, sell it, share it or use it to train any model.
  • Typing, script conversion and the other tools run on your device or in memory on our server. Translation is the one feature where your text is processed by an AI model: it goes through mcp.bhasini.com to Cloudflare Workers AI, and is not stored by us.
  • The hosted server sees your IP address and basic request details. These are used only to rate-limit and to keep the service running, and are deleted within 7 days.
  • We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising or profiling.

Hosted MCP server and ChatGPT plugin (mcp.bhasini.com)

When you use the Bhasini plugin in ChatGPT (or the Bhasini connector in Claude or another AI assistant), the assistant decides when to call a Bhasini tool and sends that request to mcp.bhasini.com. Bhasini never sees your conversation, chat history, files, name, email address or ChatGPT account. It receives only the values the assistant puts into a tool call.

Tool input text is whatever the assistant sends, so it could contain personal information if you ask for, say, your name or address to be written in another script. However, it is handled the same way as any other text: it is converted in memory, the result is returned in the response, and both are discarded. Neither the input nor the output is written to disk, a database or a log. All tools are read-only and keep no state between calls. Every tool except translate runs entirely inside our server and contacts no other service.

translate sends the text to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs the translation models (AI4Bharat IndicTrans2 and Google Gemma) and returns the result. We do not store the text or the translation. Under Cloudflare's terms, Workers AI does not use this text to train models.

An assistant may add metadata to a tool call, such as your locale or approximate location. Bhasini ignores this metadata and does not store it.

Connection data

Like any web service, the hosted server receives connection data with every request: your IP address (in ChatGPT this is usually an OpenAI server address rather than your own), the time, the URL path, the HTTP method, the response status and size, and standard headers such as the user agent. This connection data is used only to:

  • Rate-limit requests: the IP address is used as a counter key to allow 120 requests a minute per client (5 a minute for translation). The counters expire after 60 seconds.
  • Daily translation allowance: to give each user 10,000 free characters of translation a day, we keep a count of characters translated per client for the current day (India time). The counter is keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address, not the address itself, holds only a number, and is deleted shortly after midnight IST.
  • Operate and secure the service: Cloudflare Workers operational logs record request metadata (time, path, method, status, duration, errors and the request headers listed above). We use them only to find faults and abuse. They never contain tool input or output text.

Local MCP server (Bhasini.Mcp)

The local server runs on your own computer. Text that your AI assistant sends to it is processed in memory and discarded when the call returns. It collects nothing and stores nothing. Its only network request is the translate tool, which sends the text to mcp.bhasini.com and is handled as described above. The other tools make no network requests, so we receive no data from them.

Website (bhasini.com)

The editor runs entirely in your browser. Text you type, convert or open is processed on your device and is not sent to us. Documents are saved only where you choose to save them.

  • Translation: if you press Translate, the selected text (or the whole document) is sent to mcp.bhasini.com and translated by Cloudflare Workers AI, as described for the translate tool above. It is not stored. Nothing is sent unless you press Translate.
  • Preferences: your language, keyboard and display options are saved in your browser's local storage so Bhasini remembers them. They stay on your device.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages, which receives standard web request data (such as your IP address and user agent) and may log it for security under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts: the site may load Noto fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard web request data (such as your IP address).
  • Voice typing: if you press the Speak button, your browser sends your audio to its own speech recognition service (for example Google in Chrome or Microsoft in Edge). We do not receive the audio.
  • Sponsorship: if you choose to sponsor Bhasini, Razorpay processes your payment. Razorpay shares a record of the payment with us (amount, date, transaction reference and the contact details you entered, such as name, email or phone). We use this record only to confirm the payment, to reply to you and to meet accounting and tax obligations. We never receive your card or bank details.

Windows app

The app works offline and sends nothing to us, except text you choose to translate with the Translate button, which is handled as on the website. Your preferences are saved locally on your computer.

When you turn on Global mode (F9), Bhasini reads your keystrokes system-wide only to convert them into the Indian language you chose and type the result into the active window. Keystrokes are handled in memory, are not stored or logged, and are not transmitted. Global mode stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time with F9 or from the tray icon.

Translation API accounts (bhasini.com/api)

If you sign up for the Translation API, we store, in a Cloudflare D1 database:

  • Your email address and the name you entered, to identify your account and contact you about it.
  • A one-way hash of your API key, not the key itself. We cannot show your key again, only replace it.
  • Your credit balance and daily usage: the number of characters and requests per day. Never the text you translate.
  • Payment records from Razorpay: order and payment ids, pack, amount and date.
  • A one-way hash of the IP address you signed up from, to limit how many accounts one network can create each day.

The dashboard keeps your API key in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. Text you translate with your key is handled like all translation: processed in memory and not stored. To close your account and delete its data, write to us from your account's email address.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop using the plugin: disconnect or remove Bhasini in ChatGPT's settings (Apps or Plugins) at any time. ChatGPT then stops sending requests to Bhasini.
  • Control what is sent: Bhasini receives only what you ask your assistant to convert. Don't ask it to convert text you don't want sent.
  • Avoid the hosted server: install the local MCP server, which sends nothing over the internet.
  • Clear local data: clear your browser's site data for bhasini.com, or uninstall the Windows app, to remove your saved preferences.
  • Access, correction and deletion: email us to ask what data we hold about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it. Because we keep no tool text and logs expire within 7 days, in practice this applies mostly to sponsorship records. We reply within 30 days. You may also have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 or the EU GDPR, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Security and international transfers

All connections to mcp.bhasini.com and bhasini.com use HTTPS. Requests are size-limited and rate-limited. Cloudflare and GitHub run on global networks, so connection data may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States and India.

Children

Bhasini is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Apart from Translation API accounts, which are meant for developers and businesses, Bhasini has no accounts, so it holds no profile of other users, whatever their age.
